# LOTM Tarot Application

The LOTM Tarot Application provides an immersive Lord of the Mysteries-themed tarot divination and pathway exploration experience.

## Language

### Mystical & Content Domain

**Pathway**:
A mystical progression line consisting of ten sequential sequences (from Sequence 9 down to Sequence 0).
_Avoid_: Path, Class, Job

**Sequence**:
A specific rank or potion tier within a Pathway.
_Avoid_: Rank, Level, Tier, Grade

**Tarot Card**:
A symbolic card within the deck mapped to archetypes, mystical lore, and divination pathways.
_Avoid_: Playing card, Card archetype

**Reading**:
A recorded tarot consultation session where cards are drawn in a spread for a querent.
_Avoid_: Divination session, Fortune, Consultation

**Spread**:
A predefined geometric arrangement and positional mapping of drawn Tarot Cards within a Reading (e.g. Single Card, Three Card, Celtic Cross).
_Avoid_: Layout, Board, Grid, Formation

**Reading Card**:
The record of a specific Tarot Card drawn in a Reading, including its spread position and upright/reversed orientation.
_Avoid_: Drawn card, Spread card

**Querent**:
The individual or persona asking a question and receiving a Tarot Reading.
_Avoid_: Client, Customer, Seeker

**Interpretation**:
The contextual narrative and mystical guidance derived from the drawn cards in a Reading.
_Avoid_: Prediction, Fortune, Meaning

### Identity & Account Domain

**User**:
An authenticated individual with secure credentials, personal reading history, and saved preferences.
_Avoid_: Account, Profile, Member, Player

**Session**:
A bounded, cryptographically verified access authorization granted to an authenticated User.
_Avoid_: Login key, Ticket, Cookie auth, State
